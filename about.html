From Bean to Cup

Founded in 2010, Brew Haven began with a simple mission: to create an exceptional coffee experience that honors the journey from bean to cup.

Our founder, Emma Thornton, spent years traveling to coffee-growing regions around the world, building relationships with local farmers and learning about sustainable farming practices. Her passion for quality coffee and ethical sourcing became the foundation of Brew Haven.

What started as a small cart in the local farmers' market has grown into a beloved community hub where coffee lovers gather to enjoy meticulously crafted beverages and forge connections. Despite our growth, our commitment to quality, sustainability, and community remains unchanged.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Sustainability

We're committed to environmentally responsible practices throughout our supply chain, from supporting sustainable farms to minimizing waste in our cafés.

Community

We believe coffee brings people together. Our cafés are designed to be welcoming spaces where connections flourish and community bonds strengthen.

Quality

We never compromise on taste. From selecting the finest beans to training our baristas in perfecting extraction techniques, quality drives every decision.

Our Coffee Process

A journey of flavor and quality

1

Sourcing

We travel the world to find the best coffee beans, focusing on direct trade relationships with farmers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability.

2

Tasting & Selection

Our master tasters evaluate each harvest, selecting only beans that meet our exacting standards for flavor profile and quality.

3

Roasting

Small-batch roasting allows us to bring out the unique character of each bean, carefully developing complex flavors while maintaining consistency.

4

Brewing

Our highly trained baristas use precise brewing methods to highlight each coffee's distinctive qualities, ensuring a perfect cup every time.
